# Identity artwork

The SGI tube cube, shared by the native apps.

Copied unmodified from `assets/` of the irix-theme repository
(`/home/jamesb/joy/tech/irix-theme`) at commit
`202a8c0f37c165fa486f7bf40ae476efcf8bb308`:

- `cube.glb`: the mesh (quantised, 1764 vertices, 3528 triangles, no normals;
  the renderer sums face normals per vertex)
- `granite-tile.png`: the IRIX desktop granite tile, two colours, drawn
  recoloured and pixel-doubled. irix-theme's `LICENSE` says "Silicon
  Graphics' material; no licence is stated for it."

The look is a port of `irix-theme/src/elements/irix-cube-gl.js` (chrome,
studio lighting and rotation) into `crates/origami-render/src/cube.wgsl`.

## Scene photographs

`scenes/` holds the equirect images the cube reflects and `scenes.json`,
which lists them, carries each one's credit line, gives each a time of day
and local hours (`origami_cube_scene_for_time` picks the one About shows) and
the order `origami_cube_next_scene` steps through. A new scene is a
1024 by 512 WebP and a new entry; the build embeds them.

Each is a freely licensed photograph from Wikimedia Commons, cropped, laid
round the horizon and mirror-tiled over 70 degrees of latitude, with sky and
ground faded from the photograph's own top and bottom rows, in the design
pass. The Commons originals are not kept here. The credit line must be shown in the
app's acknowledgements.

## Machine stills

`stills/` holds machine-models' product stills for the machines Origami
offers, shared by the three apps. Copied from the hinv-archive repository
(`/home/jamesb/joy/tech/hinv-archive`, `art/publish/stills/<id>/`):

- `front34-{512,256,128}.png` unchanged from branch `origami/product-stills`,
  commit `28b4ceac034825fed0d30020b788d1c62de24ab9`;
- `front34-high-{512,256,128}.png` unchanged from branch
  `stills-front34-high`, commit `8b5b40fa449534ec15516ac579531b25049fc890`,
  for the flat machines (Indy, Origin 300, Origin 350, Onyx 350).

`<pt>-{light,dark}[@2x].png` (20, 34, 48 and 72 pt) are generated by
`tools/identity-stills.py` with the small-size treatment in the design's
identity addendum. Flat machines draw them from `front34-high`.
`card[@2x].png` (120 by 90 pt) and `hero[@2x].png` (240 by 180 pt) frame the
front34 still at equal weight, shadow kept. Rerunning the
script on the two commits above reproduces every file.

`manifest.json` maps Origami topologies and products to a still and carries
each still's colour. A colour is the median of the body pixels (opaque, 40th
to 80th luminance percentile) of `front34-512.png`. It is sampled from the
render, not read from hinv's look files.

The apps show every still as it is, with no badge for hinv's review status.
The status is recorded here and in `manifest.json` only. Several stills show a
different machine variant than the one an Origami topology selects (for
example Origin 200 variants all use the tower, and Onyx 3000 uses the short
rack V-brick still), because hinv has no closer one.
